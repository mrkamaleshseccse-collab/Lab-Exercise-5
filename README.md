# AIM 
To identify user problems in two different UX project scenarios, formulate clear UX problem statements, select suitable user research methods, and create user personas based on user needs, goals, behaviors, pain points, and motivations.

## ALGORITHM / PROCEDURE
Select two different UX project scenarios.
Identify the target users for each scenario.
Identify the major user pain points.
Convert the pain points into UX problem statements.
Select suitable research methods.
Collect or consider user information such as goals and behaviors.
Create at least two user personas.
Identify the goals, behaviors, pain points, and motivations of each persona.
Compare the research findings and identify possible UX improvements.
Present the final scenarios, research methods, and personas.
## OUTPUT
1. IDENTIFYING AND WRITING PROBLEM STATEMENTS
UX PROJECT SCENARIO 1 – HEALTHCARE APP
User Pain Points
Users forget to take medicines on time.
Users find it difficult to manage multiple medicines.
Health records are difficult to organize.
Users may miss appointment dates.
Too many notifications can confuse users.
Complicated navigation makes the app difficult to use.
Users may have difficulty finding important health information.
Problem Statement 1
A patient needs a simple way to track medicines because reminders and health records are difficult to manage, which results in missed doses and poor health management.

2. UX PROJECT SCENARIO 2 – E-COMMERCE APP
User Pain Points
Users find it difficult to choose from too many products.
Searching for products takes too much time.
Product information may not be clear.
Users are unsure about product quality.
Users have difficulty comparing products.
Delivery status may not be clearly displayed.
The checkout process may be complicated.
Users may abandon their purchase due to confusion.
Problem Statement 1
An online shopper needs a quick way to find and track products because too many choices and unclear order information create confusion, which results in abandoned purchases.

3. IDENTIFYING APPROPRIATE RESEARCH METHODS
3.1 Research Methods for Healthcare App
1. User Interviews
Conduct one-to-one interviews with patients to understand their problems with medicine reminders, appointments, and managing health records.

2. Surveys
Give questionnaires to a larger group of users to collect information about their health-app usage, preferred features, and common difficulties.

3. Usability Testing
Ask users to perform tasks such as setting a medicine reminder or checking a health record. This helps identify navigation and usability problems.

Most Suitable Methods
User interviews and usability testing are the most suitable methods because interviews help understand user needs and problems, while usability testing helps identify difficulties while using the application.

3.2 Research Methods for E-Commerce App
1. User Interviews
Interview online shoppers to understand their problems while searching for products, comparing prices, reading reviews, and completing purchases.

2. Surveys
Collect responses from different customers about their shopping habits, preferred features, product preferences, and common problems.

3. Usability Testing
Ask users to search for a product, add it to the cart, make a purchase, and track the order. This helps identify problems in the shopping process.

Most Suitable Methods
Surveys and usability testing are the most suitable methods because surveys provide information about common shopping problems, while usability testing helps identify specific usability issues in the shopping process.
